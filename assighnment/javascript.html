The Dark Knight (2008) follows Batman as he works with Lieutenant Jim Gordon and District Attorney Harvey Dent to dismantle organized crime in Gotham City. Their efforts are challenged by the Joker, a chaotic criminal mastermind who manipulates the city's criminals and terrorizes its citizens. As the Joker's attacks escalate, he forces Batman into difficult moral choices and exposes the limits of justice and order. Harvey Dent is tragically transformed by personal loss and the Joker's schemes, becoming the vengeful Two-Face. In the end, Batman takes responsibility for Dent's crimes to preserve hope in Gotham, becoming a hunted hero despite his sacrifices.
